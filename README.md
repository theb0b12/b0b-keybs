# b0b-keybs

These are keyboards that I have designed (sometimes with the help of other people),  including keyboards I make for commissions for other people (noted with a (C)). These are entirely my own designs, all are open source under the AGPL v3 license. I have not included just pcbs or other parts of designs that I have as commissions. Only full keyboard builds here :)

While I would like other people to not use these designs for commercial gain, I also believe in FULLY open source, unlike what most people do which is CC BY-NC 4.0 or some other creative commons license. So you do what ever you want with these, using the AGPL license.
